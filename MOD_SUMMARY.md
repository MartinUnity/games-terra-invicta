# Zethrok — Terra Invicta "Long War" Mod

## Overview

**Zethrok** is a content-expansion mod for Terra Invicta designed to stretch the game into a marathon-length experience. By introducing over 1,100 new research projects and dozens of new ship systems, it transforms a typical campaign into a deep, multi-layered strategic struggle where technology trees feel expansive and progression meaningful.

## How to Play

For best results, start your game with a **reduced progress speed** setting (10%, 15%, or 20% of normal). The mod adds a significant amount of research content — at normal speed the tree gets through too quickly. Slowing down gives the new content room to breathe and makes each technological breakthrough feel earned.

## What's Added

* **1,128 new research projects** spanning all eight tech categories — Military Science, Social Science, Materials, Space Science, Energy, Xenology, Information Science, and Life Science
* **25 new tech gates** that create meaningful branching points in the research tree
* **78 new weapons** including lasers, plasma cannons, particle/ion arrays, magnetic guns, conventional guns, and missiles
* **102 new drives** expanding ship propulsion options from basic thrusters to advanced fusion and exotic systems
* **12 new power plants** with varying output and efficiency profiles
* **5 new armor types and 6 new hull designs** for ship customization
* **11 new hab modules** including mining/refining stations, automated power plants, and point defense arrays

## Resource & Infrastructure Overhaul

The mod dramatically shifts the resource landscape. Base game mining caps are expanded by **hundreds of percent** through tiered mining bonuses (water, volatiles, metals, nobles, fissiles) and global space mining improvements. What starts as a measured build-up quickly becomes a ruthless scramble between all factions for the best mining real estate — every habitat and station becomes a prize worth fighting over.

* **Tiered mining bonuses** — Six levels per resource type (2.5% → 18% each), stackable across projects, pushing total output multipliers well beyond 200%
* **Refinery upgrades** — Flat percentage boosts per building level, plus new cascade extraction processing that compounds yields across all resource types
* **Automated platform improvements** — Specialized automation refineries, upgraded power plants, and a compact point-defense station so automated outposts can finally defend themselves against hostile incursions

## New Weapon Families

* **Plasma Weapons** — 21 new entries across prototype arrays, prototype cannons, and phased plasma systems
* **Laser Weapons** — 29 new entries including rapid-fire batteries (40cm) and heavy cannons (480cm, 720cm) across IR, green, and UV spectra
* **Conventional Guns** — 17 new ballistic and electromagnetic railgun variants
* **Particle/Ion** — 4 new charged-particle beam weapons
